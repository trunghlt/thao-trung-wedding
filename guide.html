Getting There & Around

Da Nang International Airport (DAD)

Da Nang Airport is Vietnam's third-largest international hub, with direct flights from major Asian cities. The airport is about 5 km from downtown and 7 km from My Khe Beach (where Furama Resort is located).

  • Airport transfers: Taxis and ride-hailing apps (Grab, Gojek) are readily available. Expect 10–15 minutes to the beach area.
  • Visa requirements: Check Vietnam visa rules for your nationality. Many visitors can enter visa-free for short stays or obtain e-visas online.

Getting Around Da Nang

  • Grab & Gojek: These ride-hailing apps work like Uber and are the easiest way to get around with kids. Drivers are plentiful and fares are affordable.
  • Taxis: Metered taxis are widely available. Look for Mai Linh and Vinasun, two reputable companies.
  • Hotel shuttles: Many resorts, including Furama, offer shuttle services to nearby attractions.
  • Walking: The My Khe Beach strip is pedestrian-friendly, with a scenic beachfront promenade perfect for morning or evening strolls.

Pro tip for families: Download the Grab app before you arrive and set up payment. It's the most reliable and transparent way to travel with children and luggage.

Plan Ahead

Weather & What to Pack

Late December is one of the best times to visit Da Nang! The weather is warm and mostly dry, perfect for families exploring the coast.

What to Expect

  • Temperature: Daytime highs around 24–28°C (75–82°F), cooling to 20–22°C (68–72°F) in the evenings.
  • Conditions: Generally sunny with occasional light showers. The sea is usually calm and swimmable.
  • Humidity: Moderate and comfortable compared to Vietnam's summer months.

Packing Essentials

  • Lightweight summer clothes (shorts, dresses, breathable fabrics)
  • Light sweater or jacket for air-conditioned spaces and cooler evenings
  • Swimwear and sun protection (hats, sunglasses, reef-safe sunscreen)
  • Comfortable walking shoes for exploring temples and markets
  • Light rain jacket or travel umbrella (just in case)

For the wedding: The ceremony is outdoors at 4:00 PM. Expect warm sunshine transitioning to a comfortable evening. Resort elegant attire is recommended.

Beaches & Water Activities

My Khe Beach

Named by Forbes as one of the most beautiful beaches on the planet, My Khe stretches right along the resort area. It's wide, clean, and perfect for families.

  • Swimming: Gentle waves and lifeguards on duty during the day. Ideal for children.
  • Beach time: Sunrise and late afternoon are magical times to visit when the light is golden and the sand is cooler.
  • Amenities: Loungers, umbrellas, beach cafés, and fresh coconut vendors.

Furama Resort Pools & Beach

Our wedding venue has direct beach access plus beautiful pools. Perfect for families who want a relaxing day without traveling.

Non Nuoc Beach

Just south of My Khe, this quieter stretch is equally beautiful and less crowded. Great if you're looking for a more peaceful beach day.

Safety note: Always swim in designated areas with lifeguard coverage. The sea is generally calm in late December, but respect any warning flags.

Explore

Cultural Sites & Family Attractions

Marble Mountains (Ngũ Hành Sơn)

15 minutes from Furama · Half-day visit

Five limestone hills dotted with Buddhist caves, temples, and spectacular viewpoints. Kids will love exploring the caves and climbing to the summit for panoramic views of the coast.

  • Family tip: There's an elevator to reduce climbing for young children and grandparents.
  • What to see: Huyen Khong Cave (the most impressive), Linh Ung Pagoda, stone carving workshops at the base.
  • Timing: Go early morning (7–9 AM) to avoid the midday heat.

Linh Ung Pagoda & Son Tra Peninsula

20 minutes from Furama · Half-day trip

Home to the towering 67-meter Lady Buddha statue overlooking the sea, this peaceful peninsula offers stunning coastal drives and hiking trails through tropical forest.

  • Great for: Multi-generational visits — scenic views without strenuous activity.
  • Wildlife: Keep an eye out for rare red-shanked douc langurs in the forest!

Ba Na Hills & Golden Bridge

1 hour from Da Nang · Full-day trip

The iconic Golden Bridge held by giant stone hands is one of Vietnam's most photographed landmarks. Ba Na Hills is a French colonial-era hill station turned theme park, reached by one of the world's longest cable car rides.

  • Perfect for: Older kids and teens who enjoy theme parks, gardens, and unique photo ops.
  • Note for families: It's a long day with lots of walking. The cable car ride is spectacular but may not suit very young children or those uncomfortable with heights.
  • Weather: Much cooler at altitude (bring layers), and it can be foggy even when Da Nang is sunny.

Dragon Bridge (Cầu Rồng)

Downtown Da Nang · Evening visit

This stunning dragon-shaped bridge lights up nightly and breathes fire and water on weekend evenings (Saturday and Sunday at 9:00 PM). Free to watch and kids love it!

Day Trip

Hoi An Ancient Town

35 minutes south of Da Nang · Full-day or evening visit

A UNESCO World Heritage Site, Hoi An is a beautifully preserved trading port with lantern-lit streets, riverside cafés, and family-friendly tailors, markets, and workshops.

What to Do

  • Old Town stroll: Wander the pedestrian streets, cross the Japanese Covered Bridge, and admire the colorful lanterns.
  • Lantern-making: Many shops offer hands-on workshops where kids can make their own silk lanterns.
  • Boat rides: Rent a paddleboat or take a sunset cruise on the Thu Bon River.
  • An Bang Beach: A quick bike ride or taxi from Hoi An's center — a beautiful, laid-back beach perfect for families.
  • Shopping: Custom-made clothes and shoes can be tailored in 24 hours (great souvenirs for the whole family!).

Best Time to Visit

Late afternoon through evening is magical when thousands of lanterns illuminate the town. The Old Town is pedestrian-only and very stroller-friendly.

Getting there: Grab/taxi is easy and affordable (~30 minutes). Many hotels also offer Hoi An shuttle services.

Eat & Explore

Dining & Local Markets

Family-Friendly Dining

Vietnamese cuisine is flavorful, fresh, and generally very kid-friendly. Don't miss these local favorites:

  • Pho: Vietnam's famous noodle soup — mild, comforting, and available everywhere.
  • Banh mi: Crispy baguette sandwiches with savory fillings.
  • Fresh spring rolls: Light, healthy, and fun for kids to help assemble.
  • Seafood: Da Nang's coastal location means incredibly fresh fish, prawns, and crab.
  • Tropical fruit: Mango, dragon fruit, rambutan — visit morning markets for the freshest selections.

Con Market (Chợ Cồn)

Downtown Da Nang

A bustling local market perfect for experiencing everyday Vietnamese life. Great for breakfast (bánh mì, fresh fruit smoothies) and souvenir shopping. Go in the morning when it's most lively.

Han Market (Chợ Hàn)

Central Da Nang

A larger covered market with everything from spices and snacks to clothes and handicrafts. The surrounding streets have excellent street food stalls.

Food safety for families: Stick to busy stalls with high turnover, drink bottled water, and your resort can recommend trusted local restaurants. Most hotels and resorts also have kids' menus with familiar options.

More Ideas

Easy Resort & Relaxation Days

With the wedding and holiday travel, your family may want some low-key days. Here are great options that don't require leaving the resort area:

  • Pool days: Many Da Nang resorts have kids' pools, waterslides, and swim-up bars for parents.
  • Spa time: Treat yourself to a massage while kids are in supervised activities or with family.
  • Beach sunrise or sunset: My Khe Beach is magical at golden hour and just steps away.
  • Resort activities: Check with your hotel for cooking classes, beach volleyball, or cultural performances.
  • Beachfront dining: Many restaurants along the beach strip offer fresh seafood with ocean views.

A Few More Tips

  • Language: English is widely spoken in tourist areas, hotels, and restaurants. Learning a few Vietnamese phrases (xin chào = hello, cảm ơn = thank you) is appreciated!
  • Currency: Vietnamese Dong (VND). ATMs are plentiful, and many places accept credit cards. US dollars are sometimes accepted but dong gives better value.
  • Dress code: Casual and comfortable. When visiting temples, cover shoulders and knees out of respect.
  • Tipping: Not obligatory but appreciated (5–10% at restaurants, small amounts for hotel staff).
